Important information

Investment Risk

Investments in independent film and television production companies are high-risk, speculative and potentially illiquid. Investors may lose some or all of their invested capital.

Shares in private companies may be difficult or impossible to sell, there may be no established secondary market, and investors may be required to hold their investments for an indefinite period.

Commercial performance depends on numerous factors, including successful production, distribution, audience demand, revenue generation and wider market conditions. None of these outcomes can be guaranteed.

Investor Eligibility

The investment opportunities described are intended only for individuals who qualify as sophisticated investors or high-net-worth investors under the applicable UK financial-promotion rules or statutory exemptions as described below.

High Net Worth Individual: You have an annual income to the value of £170,000 or more, or; net assets (excluding pension/primary residence) of £430,000 or more.

Sophisticated Investor: In the last two years you have been the director of a company with an annual turnover of at least £1.6 million, or; you have been a member of a network or syndicate of business angels for at least six months, or; you have worked in the past two years in a professional capacity in the private equity sector or in the provision of finance for small and medium enterprises.

*Enterprise Investment Scheme (EIS)

Certain investments may qualify for tax relief under the Enterprise Investment Scheme, subject to the relevant company, share issue and individual investor satisfying HMRC requirements.

The availability and value of any tax relief depend on individual circumstances and may change. EIS relief is not guaranteed, and relief previously obtained may be withdrawn if the applicable conditions are not maintained.

Investors should obtain independent professional tax advice before making an investment decision.

Role of Datamine Promotions

Datamine Promotions is a data capture company. It collects enquiries from individuals interested in the British film and television industry and, with their consent, passes their contact details to independent film and television production companies.

Datamine Promotions does not provide personalised investment advice, manage investor funds or receive investment capital on behalf of production companies.

Investment decisions, subscription arrangements and contractual obligations are entered into directly between investors and the relevant independent production company.

Nothing in this disclaimer constitutes a representation that Datamine Promotions is authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.
