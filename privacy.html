This policy explains how Soho Investment Group collects and uses your personal information when you use this website and complete our questionnaire, including how we share your contact details with film and entertainment companies about the investment opportunities you are interested in.

1. Who we are

Soho Investment Group is a trading name of [company legal name], a company registered in England and Wales (company number [number]), with its registered office at [registered address]. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration number [ICO number].

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at [privacy email address] or write to us at the address above.

2. The information we collect

When you complete our questionnaire and enquiry form, we collect:

  • Your contact details: first name, last name, email address and phone number.
  • Your questionnaire answers: your answers to our five questions, the investment avenue we suggested, and the avenue or avenues you asked for information on.
  • Your consent: a record that you agreed to us sharing your details, and when.
  • How you found us: the web page you were on and, if you arrived from an advert, the campaign details in the link (for example the advert's source and campaign name).
  • Technical information: our website host and service providers automatically receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit.

3. How we use your information

  • To match you with the film and TV investment avenue that best suits your interests.
  • To pass your contact details and investment interests to the relevant film and entertainment companies (see section 4), so they can contact you about their investment opportunities.
  • To contact you ourselves by email and phone about the opportunities you asked about.
  • To keep records of your enquiry and consent, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To understand which of our adverts and pages are working, so we can improve them.

4. Sharing your details with film and entertainment companies

With your consent, we share your name, email address, phone number and the investment avenue(s) you are interested in with film and entertainment companies that offer investment opportunities in those avenues. This may include production companies, post-production companies, audio visual and screening companies, hair and make-up companies, and film festival organisers.

If you choose "open to everything", we may share your details with companies across all six avenues.

Once these companies receive your details, they will use them to contact you by email and phone about their investment opportunities. They become responsible for your information under their own privacy policies, which they should give you when they first contact you.

We only share your details with companies relevant to the avenues you chose, and we do not sell your information for any other purpose.

5. Our legal reasons for using your information

  • Consent: we rely on your consent, given by ticking the box on our form, to share your details with the companies described in section 4 and to contact you by email and phone about investment opportunities.
  • Legitimate interests: we use your information to keep records of enquiries, improve our website and adverts, and protect our business, where this does not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: we keep certain records where the law or our regulators require it.

6. Our service providers

We use trusted providers to run this website and manage enquiries. They only use your information on our instructions:

  • HubSpot (customer relationship management): stores your enquiry details. Our HubSpot account is hosted in the European Union.
  • Netlify (website hosting): serves this website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts used on this website, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK's adequacy regulations or approved contract terms.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep your enquiry details for [24 months] from our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we need to keep them longer to meet legal or regulatory requirements.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete your information;
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use your information;
  • ask us to transfer your information to another organisation.

To use any of these rights, or to stop us contacting you, email [privacy email address]. If you withdraw consent, we will stop sharing your details from that point and will tell the companies we have already shared them with, where we can. You can also ask those companies directly to stop contacting you.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
