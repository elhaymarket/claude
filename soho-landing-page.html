Capital at risk. Investments in film and television are high risk. You could lose all of the money you invest, and you should not expect to be protected if something goes wrong. These investments may be difficult to sell, and past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Nothing on this website is personal advice. *Tax relief. Enterprise Investment Scheme (EIS) Income Tax Relief of 30% is available only on investments in companies that meet HMRC's EIS rules, and only if those rules continue to be met. Relief depends on your individual circumstances, may be withdrawn if the company or investor stops qualifying, and tax rules can change. Shares must normally be held for at least three years to keep the relief. Please speak to a tax adviser.
